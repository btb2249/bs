Privacy Policy — BS

Last updated: November 23, 2026

BS is made by Bethel Ventures. BS is Bret & Shelby's clothing line. This policy explains, in plain words, what the app does with your information.

The short version: the BS app collects nothing. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no server. The app does not collect, sell, share, or send your personal information anywhere.

How the app works

  • The app is a storefront and shirt builder: you pick a back design, a shirt color, and a front print color, and the app shows you the combo on your phone. Your picks are not saved by the app after you leave and are never sent anywhere.
  • No orders or payments are taken yet. Ordering is not open — the print shop is still being connected. The order buttons in the app say so plainly. No payment information is asked for, collected, or stored, and no purchase can be made in this version.
  • When ordering does open, shirts will be printed one at a time by the print shop when an order is placed. If that changes how the app handles information, this policy will be updated first (see Changes below).

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell anything yet and takes no payments of any kind.
  • It does not access your contacts, location, photos, microphone, or messages.
  • It does not show ads and contains no advertising or tracking software.

Children: BS is made for adults. It is not directed at children.

Changes: If a future version changes how the app handles information — including any version where ordering is switched on — this policy will be updated and the new version posted at the same address before the change takes effect.
